07 / PROBLEM SOLVING

CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

During the production of STORAGE, I faced several challenges that required different solutions.

01 / HAIR

One of the main challenges was learning how to create hair using Blender's grooming tools. However, the goal was not simply to make the hair look realistic, but to achieve a stylized appearance that matched the character's overall design. I also had to pay special attention to the hair roots, making sure they blended naturally with the character's head and did not look awkward or out of place.

02 / EXTRA MASK

I wanted to create two different versions of the character: one without Bonnie's mask and another wearing it. This introduced several technical challenges. Inside STORAGE's rig, I created a button that allowed me to switch between the character's normal head rig and the mask rig. This was particularly challenging because both versions had completely different eye setups: STORAGE had regular 3D eyes, while Bonnie's cartoon-style eyes were represented by flat planes that changed textures depending on the position of the eye bones. The switching system also had to control the character's hair, hiding it whenever the mask was enabled so that it would not interfere with the mask's appearance. Finally, the mask and its rig had to appear and disappear together. The button was designed to allow animators to switch between both configurations at any point during the animation, without having to manually change the rig setup.

03 / BLEND SHAPES

Another important challenge was creating the facial expressions for STORAGE. I had to build a set of blend shapes specifically for the character's face, allowing it to display different emotions and expressions. These blend shapes were also essential for creating vocal shapes for lip-sync, making it possible to animate the character's mouth and facial movements to match spoken dialogue. The goal was to create a flexible facial setup that could support both expressive acting and speech animation.

04 / CLIPPING TEXTURES

Another challenge was managing the different clothing layers of the character. STORAGE has a complete body underneath his clothes, a T-shirt, and a long coat that reaches down to his knees. This created the problem of clothing and body parts intersecting or becoming visible when they shouldn't. To solve this, I created a system that allowed specific parts of the character to become transparent or disappear whenever they were not needed. I also added a button to the rig, similar to the one used for the mask, allowing these parts to be shown or hidden whenever necessary. This system made it possible to switch between different clothing configurations, giving STORAGE several outfit variations while keeping the character's appearance clean.
